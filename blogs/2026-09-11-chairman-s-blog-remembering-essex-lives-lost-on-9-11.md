Chairman's Blog: Remembering Essex Lives Lost on 9/11 Ellis Whitehou… Fri, 09/11/2026 - 12:31 11 September 2026 Today, on the anniversary of the terrorist attacks of 11 September 2001, we pause to remember the thousands of innocent people who lost their lives in one of the darkest moments in modern history. Twenty-five years on, the events of that day continue to resonate across the world, reminding us of both the devastating consequences of hatred and the enduring strength of communities united in grief and remembrance. For Essex, this is not only a moment to reflect on a global tragedy.
